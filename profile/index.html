About

I am a Senior Software Engineer with over 15 years of experience building system-level software across Android and Linux platforms.

My career began in low-level embedded systems, developing drivers and middleware for RTOS and Linux-based automotive products, and evolved toward Android platform and AOSP development, including system services, HALs, and native components.

Experienced in Android platform and AOSP development, system services, HALs, and native applications using C++ and Java. Strong background in end-to-end product delivery and automated testing.

I also specialize in designing and implementing robust platform software using C++, Java, and Python, with a strong focus on performance, reliability, and automated testing. I have delivered production software across the full product lifecycle in automotive and consumer electronics domains.
